Our Products

What is Carrageenan?

Carrageenan is a natural substance derived from red seaweed. It serves as a thickener, gelling agent, and stabilizer across countless applications — from food and beverages to pharmaceuticals and cosmetics.

01

Kappa Carrageenan

Forms strong, rigid gels in the presence of potassium ions. Ideal for dairy products, meat processing, and water-based gels. Provides excellent synergy with locust bean gum.

  • Processed meats & poultry
  • Dairy desserts & yogurts
  • Water jellies & desserts
  • Toothpaste & personal care
02

Iota Carrageenan

Forms soft, elastic gels with calcium ions. Perfect for applications requiring freeze-thaw stability and smooth texture. Excellent for dairy and suspension applications.

  • Milk beverages & shakes
  • Ice cream & frozen desserts
  • Sauces & gravies
  • Pet food formulations
03

Lambda Carrageenan

Fully soluble and does not gel. Acts as a thickener and stabilizer. Preferred for applications requiring viscosity and suspension without gel formation.

  • Chocolate milk & beverages
  • Cream & creamers
  • Beer & wine clarification
  • Cosmetic formulations

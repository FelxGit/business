Sports Program

Promoting physical fitness and teamwork

Home Sports Program

Launched in 2006, the DASH Sports Program is funded and supervised by the company but managed by elected employee officers. This ensures the inclusion of sports activities that truly interest our employees, fostering genuine engagement and enthusiasm.

We believe that a healthy body contributes to a healthy mind. Our sports program encourages physical fitness, teamwork, and healthy competition — values that translate directly into our professional work.

Sports Activities:

  • Basketball leagues and tournaments
  • Volleyball competitions
  • Badminton and table tennis
  • Running and fun run events
  • Company sports festivals
  • Inter-department competitions
  • Participation in external corporate sports events

DASH Engineering Philippines Inc. is a 100% subsidiary of Mitsui E&S Engineering.

Welcome to DASH Engineering

Over 25 years of existence, DASH works hand in hand with Mitsui E&S Engineering in providing quality services at competitive costs and creditable technology.

Our Legacy

Established in 1992, DASH has grown from a 10-person team to a world-class engineering firm serving international clients across the globe. We are the only Philippine subsidiary of Mitsui E&S.

Our Expertise

We specialize in three core engineering divisions: Plant Engineering, Shipbuilding, and Advanced Machinery — utilizing cutting-edge 2D/3D technologies and world-class methodologies.

Our Commitment

ISO 9001:2000 certified for Quality Management and ISO/IEC 27001:2005 certified for Information Security. We maintain the highest standards in every project we undertake.

Three flagship divisions delivering world-class engineering solutions

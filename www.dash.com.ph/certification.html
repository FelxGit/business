Our Certifications

DASH Engineering maintains the highest standards of quality and security through internationally recognized certifications.

ISO 9001:2000

Plant Engineering - Quality Management System

Acquired in 2004, this certification validates our commitment to delivering consistent, high-quality engineering design services in plant engineering. It demonstrates our adherence to internationally recognized quality management principles.

Acquired: 2004

ISO 9001:2000

Shipbuilding - Quality Management System

Acquired in 2005, this certification extends our quality management excellence to our shipbuilding division. It ensures that our ship design and engineering processes meet rigorous international quality standards.

Acquired: 2005

ISO/IEC 27001:2005

Information Security Management System (ISMS)

Acquired in 2008, DASH became the first engineering company in the Philippines to achieve this certification. It validates our best practices in managing information security, protecting both our data and our clients' sensitive information.

Acquired: 2008

PEZA Certification

Philippine Economic Zone Authority

Acquired in 2005, this certification allows DASH to operate within Philippine economic zones, enabling us to serve international clients with preferential trade benefits while contributing to the Philippine economy.

Acquired: 2005

DASH Engineering Philippines Inc. is a 100% subsidiary of Mitsui E&S Engineering, providing world-class plant engineering, shipbuilding, and advanced machinery design services for over 25 years.

Privacy Policy

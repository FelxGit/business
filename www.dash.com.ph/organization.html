Organizational Structure

DASH Engineering Philippines Inc. operates under a well-defined organizational structure designed to support our three core business divisions: Plant Engineering, Shipbuilding, and Advanced Machinery. As a 100% subsidiary of Mitsui E&S Engineering, we maintain close alignment with our parent company's global standards while adapting to the local Philippine business environment.

Our organization is led by the President, supported by division managers overseeing each core business area. The structure promotes efficient communication, rapid decision-making, and seamless collaboration across departments. Each division is staffed with experienced engineers, designers, and support personnel who bring diverse expertise to every project.
